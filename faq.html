Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions.

How do I place an order?

Browse the store, select a product and add it to your cart. Continue to checkout and submit your order.

What payment methods do you accept?

AfriGadgets currently accepts bank transfer and PayShap payments. Payment instructions are provided during checkout.

How do I send proof of payment?

After placing your order, use the proof-of-payment section to upload your payment confirmation.

How long does delivery take?

Delivery times depend on your location and the product ordered. See our Delivery page for more information.

Can I cancel an order?

Contact our support team as soon as possible if you need to cancel or change an order.

How can I contact AfriGadgets?

You can contact us through our Contact page.
