# AfriGadgets installment eligibility

Installments are available for eligible larger-ticket products priced at R2,000 or more.

Small accessories / low-ticket items are excluded, including kettles, earphones/earbuds/headphones, chargers, cables, adapters, mice, keyboards, flash drives, memory cards, hubs, cases, covers, styluses, screen protectors, remote controls, extension leads and card readers.

Larger products such as phones, laptops, tablets, TVs, fridges, generators, solar equipment, gaming consoles, electric cars and qualifying kitchen appliances remain eligible when priced at R2,000 or more.

The storefront checks the product name/category before showing the installment button.
