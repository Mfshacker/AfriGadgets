Refund & Returns Policy

Last updated: 12/04/2026

At AfriGadgets, we want you to be happy with your gadget purchase. If you experience a problem with a gadget or anything else in your order, please contact us so that we can assist you.

Returns

We accept eligible returns within 30 days of receiving your gadget.

To be eligible for a return, your gadget should be:

  • In the same condition in which you received it.
  • Unused and undamaged, unless the return relates to a defective or damaged gadget.
  • In its original packaging where reasonably possible.
  • Accompanied by proof of purchase, such as an order number, receipt, or confirmation email.
  • Complete with all accessories, cables, chargers, manuals and other items supplied with the gadget, where applicable.

To request a return, contact us at returns@afrigadgets.co.za .

Please do not send a gadget back without contacting AfriGadgets first. We will provide you with the appropriate return instructions.

Damaged, Defective or Incorrect Gadgets

Please inspect your order as soon as you receive it.

If your gadget arrives damaged, defective, incomplete, or different from what you ordered, please contact us as soon as possible, preferably within 7 days of delivery.

Please provide your order number together with photographs or other relevant evidence of the issue so that we can properly assess the situation.

Where appropriate, AfriGadgets may arrange a repair, replacement, refund or other applicable remedy.

Exceptions / Non-Returnable Gadgets

Certain gadgets or accessories may not be eligible for return where they have been used, opened or altered in a manner that makes them unsuitable for resale, subject to your rights under applicable law.

Examples may include:

  • Personal-use gadgets or accessories where hygiene considerations apply.
  • Gadgets damaged through misuse, accidental damage or improper installation.
  • Gadgets that have been modified, repaired or tampered with without authorization.
  • Digital products, software or downloadable content once accessed, where applicable.
  • Gift cards or vouchers, where applicable.

Sale or promotional gadgets are not automatically excluded from your statutory consumer rights. If a discounted gadget is defective or otherwise qualifies for a remedy under applicable law, we will assist you accordingly.

Gadget Exchanges

If you would like to exchange a gadget for another gadget, please contact AfriGadgets first.

Where an exchange is approved, the replacement gadget will be subject to stock availability. If the replacement gadget has a different price, any applicable price difference will be communicated before the exchange is completed.

Refunds

Once we receive and inspect your returned gadget, we will notify you whether your refund has been approved.

If your refund is approved, the refund will generally be processed using the original payment method, where possible.

Please allow up to 10 business days for AfriGadgets to process an approved refund. Your bank or payment provider may require additional time before the funds appear in your account.

If you have not received your refund after the applicable processing period, please contact: refunds@afrigadgets.co.za

Order Cancellations

If you need to cancel an order containing one or more gadgets, please contact AfriGadgets as soon as possible.

We will attempt to accommodate cancellation requests before the order has been dispatched. Once an order has already been dispatched, cancellation may no longer be possible and the applicable returns process may apply.

Your Consumer Rights

Nothing in this Refund & Returns Policy is intended to limit, exclude or waive any rights you may have under the South African Consumer Protection Act, 68 of 2008 (CPA) or any other applicable law.

Where the law provides you with a right to a repair, replacement, refund, cancellation or other remedy, those rights will continue to apply to your AfriGadgets purchase.

Gadget Accessories and Components

Gadgets supplied with accessories, chargers, cables, manuals, cases or other components should be returned together with those items where applicable.

Missing accessories or components may affect the condition of a returned gadget and may be considered when assessing a return, subject to your statutory consumer rights.
